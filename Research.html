Research Vision

This report presents an interdisciplinary framework for defining theses and projects at the Faham Center. The common thread across all topics is the connection between Artificial Intelligence, computing infrastructure, energy, and distributed decision-making systems — ensuring research outcomes have both academic value and industry transferability.
